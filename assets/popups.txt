[ready]
Power off your OM-108. Remove the battery door and put the PLAY/UPDATE switch in the UPDATE position. Connect the OM-108 to your PC using a USB-B cable.

Press OK, then hold the Omnichord's power button until the log says DONE or RESULT. If it stops, just do the same again.

[full_warning]
WARNING: Full Factory Restore.

This rewrites every part of the unit's firmware that differs from your stock firmware file, whatever is on it now (also firmware this tool does not recognize), and the start-up area too. Only your unit's own saved settings at the very top are left alone (a factory reset clears them).

Use it ONLY if a normal Restore Stock Firmware fails. If it stops part way, the unit may not start normally until you run it again: the UPDATE switch always works, so just do the same again.
